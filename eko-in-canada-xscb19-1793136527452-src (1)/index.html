Our story

A little piece of Lagos, made for sharing.

Eko in Kanada was born from a simple feeling: the joy of a meal that takes you home. Founded by Bolarinwa Pearl Aina, we bring the generous spirit and unmistakable flavour of Lagos to tables across Ontario.

Every dish is prepared with care, using beloved Nigerian recipes and quality ingredients. Whether you’re celebrating with family or missing the taste of home, there’s always a seat at our table.
